01 BIO

Laura
Leal-Taixé.

I am Director of Research at NVIDIA, directing the Spatial Intelligence Lab (SIL).

I am also an Adjunct Professor at the University of Toronto. From 2018 to 2022, I was a professor at TUM. Previously, I spent two years as a postdoctoral researcher at ETH Zurich and one year as a senior postdoctoral researcher in TUM’s Computer Vision Group.

I received the Sofja Kovalevskaja Award in 2017, the Google Faculty Award in 2021, and an ERC Starting Grant in 2022.
